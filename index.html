About PalletLine Wholesale

Built to Protect the Profit Margins of Independent Shops in Northampton

Based right here in Northampton, PalletLine Wholesale was founded with a single goal: to give independent convenience stores, off-licences, cafés, takeaways and clubs direct access to fast-moving products without the inflated overheads of massive national cash-and-carries.

Because we operate a lean local distribution model out of Northampton and source high-volume pallet lines directly, we pass those savings straight on to your till. You don't have to waste hours queuing at a depot or tying up cash flow upfront—we deliver free the very next day and let you pay by Cash or Bank Transfer once your stock is at your desired location.
